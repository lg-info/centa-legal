Privacy Policy

“Centa Blocus” app · Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short. Centa Blocus does not ask for an account, an email address or a name. Your preferences and scores stay on your device. The only data sent to third parties relates to advertising, and only after your choice: you can accept personalized ads or decline them (you will then see non-personalized ads). You can change your mind at any time from the game menu.

Contact: lginfo@bbox.fr

2. What data is used?

a) Data that stays on your device

The game stores locally (browser or app storage): your chosen theme, difficulty level, your statistics (games played, wins, best score), your daily challenge streak and your choice regarding ads. This data is never sent to the publisher and is deleted if you clear the app's or the browser's data.

b) Advertising-related data

The game is free and funded by advertising, served by Google (AdMob / AdSense). When ads are displayed, Google may collect, depending on your choice:

DataNon-personalized adsPersonalized ads
IP address, device and browser type, language, countryYes (operation, measurement, fraud prevention)Yes

Interactions with ads (display, click)YesYes

Centa Blocus does not send Google any name, email address or game data. To learn more: how Google uses ad data.

3. Why (purposes) and on what legal basis?

  • Showing personalized ads: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can refuse or withdraw it with no effect on the game.
  • Showing non-personalized ads, measuring their delivery and preventing fraud: the legitimate interest of funding the free game (Art. 6(1)(f)) and, depending on the case, the security obligations of our service providers.
  • Running the game (remembering your preferences on your device): necessary for the service you requested.

4. Your choice and how to change it

When you first launch the game, a screen asks you to choose between “Accept” and “Refuse personalized ads”. Both buttons carry equal weight. No ad is loaded before you make your choice.

You can change it at any time: Menu ☰ → “Confidentialité et pubs” (Privacy and ads). On Android, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads. On the web, you can manage your preferences at adssettings.google.com.

5. Who receives the data?

Only our advertising providers: Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. No other sale or transfer of data takes place.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Google may process data outside the EU, notably in the United States. These transfers are covered by the mechanisms provided for by the GDPR (adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses). Details are in Google's terms.

7. Retention period

Local data is kept until you clear the app's or the browser's data. Data collected by Google is kept according to its own policy. Your consent choice is stored on your device and may be requested again after 12 months at most.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time. Since Centa Blocus holds no data that identifies you, these rights for advertising data are mainly exercised with Google (adssettings.google.com). For any question: lginfo@bbox.fr.

You may also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (the French data protection authority) or your local authority: cnil.fr.

9. Children and teenagers

Centa Blocus is intended for people aged 13 and over. In France, children under 15 cannot consent alone to personalized ads: a parent's agreement is required. If you are under 15, choose “Refuse personalized ads”. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13.

10. Security

The game collects no sensitive data and has no account or password. Exchanges with advertising services use an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

11. Changes

This policy may change, for example if new services are added. The update date appears at the top of the page. In case of a significant change, your consent will be requested again.
